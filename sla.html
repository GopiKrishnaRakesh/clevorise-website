2. Incident Severity Classification & Response Matrix

Technical incidents are categorized based on commercial impact to merchant operations:

Tier Definition Initial Response Target Resolution
P1 - Critical Complete system failure: customer unable to complete orders, storefront totally offline, or EPOS bridge failure during live trading. Within 1 Hour (24/7) Under 4 Hours
P2 - High Major operational impairment: automated invoicing queue stalled or inventory sync delayed across secondary channels. Within 3 Hours Under 8 Hours
P3 - Medium Minor glitch or cosmetic issue: analytics reporting discrepancy or localized layout formatting error. Within 8 Working Hours Under 24 Hours
P4 - Low General consultative request, minor configuration adjustment, or routine advisory enquiry. Within 1 Business Day Scheduled per SOW

3. Scheduled Maintenance Window

To preserve stability and deploy security patches, CLEVORISE performs maintenance during off-peak commercial hours:

  • Standard Window: Sunday mornings between 01:00 AM and 05:00 AM GMT.
  • Advance Notification: Routine maintenance is announced with a minimum of 48 hours' prior email notice.
  • Zero-Downtime Deployments: Application code updates utilize blue-green rolling deployments without interrupting customer checkouts.

4. Infrastructure Redundancy & Disaster Recovery

CLEVORISE systems are architected with enterprise-grade resilience:

  • UK Dual-Cluster Architecture: Primary clusters hosted in London with real-time failover to a secondary UK data centre.
  • Automated Backups: Point-in-time database backups performed every 6 hours and stored in encrypted, geographically isolated UK cloud storage.
  • RTO & RPO: Target Recovery Time Objective (RTO) of < 2 hours; Recovery Point Objective (RPO) of < 15 minutes.

5. Service Credits & Support Escalation

In the event that monthly verified uptime falls below 99.8% due to causes within CLEVORISE's reasonable control, clients are entitled to service fee credits:

  • 99.0% - 99.79% Availability: 10% credit applied toward the following month's platform retainer.
  • 95.0% - 98.99% Availability: 25% credit applied toward the following month's platform retainer.
  • < 95.0% Availability: 50% credit applied toward the following month's platform retainer.
